Interview Preparation Guide

Body language, answering techniques, and mindset tips.

🪑
Sitting posture
Sit straight, lean slightly forward. Feet flat on floor. Don't cross arms — it signals defensiveness.
👁️
Eye contact
Natural eye contact — not staring. For video calls, look at the camera. Blink normally.
🗣️
STAR method
Situation → Task → Action → Result. Always end with a measurable result you achieved.
⏸️
Pause before answering
"Let me think for a moment" shows thoughtfulness. A 3-second pause is perfectly fine.
😊
Smile naturally
A genuine smile builds rapport instantly. Think of something you're excited about in the role.
🎯
Research the company
Know their mission, recent news, products. Mention something specific — shows genuine interest.
❓
Ask smart questions
"What does success look like in 90 days?" Always prepare 2-3 questions to ask them.
💻
Video interview setup
Camera at eye level. Light in front. Clean background. Test audio 10 mins before.
🎙️
Voice & pace
Speak 20% slower than you think. Clear, measured speech sounds more confident than rushing.
