Interview Preparation Guide

Body language, answering techniques, and mindset tips.

🪑
Sitting posture
Sit straight, lean slightly forward. Keep both feet flat on the floor. Don't cross your arms — it signals defensiveness.
👁️
Eye contact
Maintain natural eye contact — not staring. For video calls, look at the camera, not your own face. Blink normally.
🗣️
STAR method
For behavioral questions: Situation → Task → Action → Result. Always end with a measurable result you achieved.
⏸️
Pause before answering
It's okay to say "let me think for a moment." A 3-second pause shows thoughtfulness, not weakness.
😊
Smile naturally
A genuine smile builds rapport instantly. Don't force it — think of something you're excited about in the role.
🎯
Research the company
Know their mission, recent news, and products. Mention something specific — it shows genuine interest.
❓
Ask smart questions
Always prepare 2-3 questions. "What does success look like in this role in 90 days?" is always impressive.
🎙️
Voice & pace
Speak 20% slower than you think you should. Clear, measured speech sounds more confident than rushing.
💻
Video interview setup
Camera at eye level. Light source in front of you. Clean, simple background. Test audio 10 mins before.
